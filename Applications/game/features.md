## Animation 
- The animation system animate a GameObject from one state to another. It can be used to animate the position, rotation, scale, and other properties of a GameObject. The animation system can be used to create smooth transitions between different states of a GameObject, such as moving from one position to another, rotating, or scaling up or down.

### Examples
- The camera can use the Animation system to smoothly transition between different positions and angles, creating a more dynamic and engaging experience for the player. For example, when the player enters a new area, the camera can animate to a new position that provides a better view of the environment. Additionally, the camera can animate to follow the player as they move through the game world, creating a more immersive experience.

- The camera can show objecives before the game starts or when the player dies, by animating to a position that provides a better view of the objective or the area where the player died. This can help the player understand what they need to do to progress in the game or how they died, providing a more engaging and informative experience.
